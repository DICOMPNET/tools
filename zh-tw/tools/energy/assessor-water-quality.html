📋 Water TDS Quality Assessor

Rate water quality by TDS (total dissolved solids) against threshold bands, also weighing temperature and intended use such as drinking, brewing or aquarium.

📐 計算公式
估算電導率 EC(µS/cm) = TDS(mg/L) ÷ 0.55；TDS 分級：≤50 超純水、≤100 優質純淨水、≤300 良好飲用水、≤500 合格飲用水（GB 5749-2022 限值）、≤1000 偏高不建議直飲、>1000 高鹽水不可飲用。校驗：無強校驗，水溫預設按 20℃。判據：用途疊加——飲用且 TDS >500 判「不合格（超出飲用水標準）」；純水用途且 TDS >50 建議 RO 反滲透處理。

❓ 常見問題（FAQ）

TDS 低就一定好喝嗎？
TDS 反映溶解固體總量，過低口感平淡、過高有異味；飲用水 300–600 mg/L 常見，關鍵是有害物達標。
濁度高有什麼風險？
濁度高的水易攜病原微生物且影響消毒效果，飲用水濁度須低並有微生物檢測。

Formula & Standard References

Item Formula / Rule Source / Standard
TDS and conductivity TDS correlates approximately linearly with conductivity, commonly converted with a factor of 0.5 to 0.7. WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality, 4th ed. (2017)
National limit The limit for total dissolved solids in drinking water is generally no more than 1000 mg per litre. GB 5749-2022, standards for drinking water quality
Palatability grading WHO classifies TDS by palatability into excellent, good, fair and poor categories. WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality, 4th ed. (2017)
⚠️ 使用說明與注意事項
  • TDS(總溶解固體)反映水中溶解性無機物總量，GB 5749限值≤500mg/L
  • TDS與電導率正相關，電導率≈TDS/0.55(μS/cm)
  • TDS過低(<30mg/L)幾乎不含礦物質，長期飲用需補充
  • TDS不能反映細菌、重金屬等有害物質，需配合其他指標
  • 水溫影響TDS測量值，建議在25°C標準溫度下測量

Color Vision (Ishihara) Plate Test

Read the number in each pseudo-isochromatic plate; normal color vision reads them all while red-green deficiency misreads or cannot read them. Screening tool, not diagnostic.

📋 評分解讀

正確數(共6圖)提示
5-6色覺正常(篩查層面)
4臨界，建議複查或正式檢查
0-3提示紅綠色覺異常

顯示器色彩、環境光均會影響結果。確診需用標準 Ishihara 38版或 anomaloscope 異色鏡檢查。

⚠️ 本測試為教育篩查用途，不能替代專業色覺檢查。職業體檢請以醫療機構標準檢查為準。

💡 常見使用場景

  • 閱讀假同色圖數字，按答對 plate 數判讀色覺。
  • 紅綠色盲篩查。
  • 臨界值的複查建議。
答對 11/12
正確率高，色覺基本正常；僅 1 錯可能為偶然。
答對 3/12
大量誤讀，強烈提示紅綠色覺缺陷，建議進一步色覺專項檢查。

❓ 常見問題（FAQ）

幾歲可做？
一般 4–5 歲以上能識數即可，幼兒用圖形版。
全錯一定是色盲？
也可能是不配合/不識數，需結合其他檢查確認。

Formula & Standard References

Item Formula / Rule Source / Standard
Plate design The 24-plate set mixes demonstration and numeral plates that red-green defectives misread or miss Ishihara, Tests for Colour-Blindness, 1917
Scoring threshold Four or more errors out of 24 suggest red-green deficiency, eight or more a severe defect Ishihara, Tests for Colour-Blindness, 1917
Colour basis Plates follow CIE 1931 chromaticity and confusion lines, which red-green defectives cannot separate CIE 1931 Standard Colorimetric Observer

Magnetic Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

Compute the magnetic force on a current-carrying wire from field B, current I, length L, and angle θ using F = B·I·L·sinθ.

📐 計算公式
F = BIL·sinθ，垂直時最大。 B=0.5T,I=10A,L=0.2m,θ=90° → 1 N。

Banked Curve Speed

Find the frictionless design speed for a banked curve from its radius and banking angle, where the horizontal component of the normal force supplies the centripetal force.

📐 計算公式
v = √(rg·tanθ)（無側滑）。 r=50,θ=30° → 約 16.8 m/s。

輸入彎道半徑 r、傾角 θ、g，求設計速度。

使用示例

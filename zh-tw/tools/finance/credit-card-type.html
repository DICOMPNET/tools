按字首區間判定：Visa(4)、Mastercard(51-55 / 2221-2720)、銀聯(62)、Amex(34/37)、JCB(35)、Discover(6011/65) 等

Detect the card network (Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay and more) from the number prefix, with length and Luhn checks entirely offline; no card tier is inferred.

🦿 Equestrian Performance Rating (Gait / Jumping)

An equestrian performance rater scoring from four dimensions — gait quality, jumping technique, rider posture and course accuracy — for dressage and show jumping.

跳水動作評分 = 4 名裁判評分均值 − 懲罰 × 0.5；分數按動作型別分級（不低於8 優、7–8 良、6–7 中、小於6 待改進）。

Formula & Standard References

Item Formula / Note Source / Standard
Dressage scoring each movement scores zero to ten and is multiplied by a coefficient FEI Dressage Rules
Jumping penalties a knocked rail costs four penalties, refusals and time add more FEI Jumping Rules
Time penalties exceeding the allowed time adds a fixed penalty per second FEI Jumping Rules
⚠️ 使用說明與注意事項
  • 馬術比賽採用10分制，8分以上為優秀
  • 盛裝舞步注重步態質量和騎手姿態
  • 障礙賽以罰分最少為勝，碰落障礙杆罰4分
  • 馬匹與騎手的默契配合是馬術的核心
  • 本工具純前端執行，資料不會上傳到伺服器

❓ 常見問題（FAQ）

罰分來源？
超時、拒跳、碰杆均罰分。
人馬配合怎麼評？
看指令響應與動作流暢度，主觀量表需固定標準以減評分者偏差。
